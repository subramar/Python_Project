Vault EDC study build · Mission

Learn Vault EDC study building

Translate existing EDC build experience into confident, accurate interview answers.

Why

Land a clinical study builder role working in Veeva Vault EDC. The learner has already built studies in another EDC; the goal is to turn that experience into fluent, credible Vault EDC answers in an interview within the next two days.

Success looks like

  • Explain the Vault EDC study design hierarchy—Study → Casebook Definition → Event Group → Event → Form → Item Group → Item—and how it becomes a subject's casebook, using Veeva's terminology.
  • Map prior EDC build experience to the Vault feature that does the same job, and explain where the systems differ.
  • Read and write a basic Vault rule expression (#define, @Form vs $ identifiers) and choose an appropriate rule action.
  • Describe the change lifecycle: validate → publish → deploy → new casebook version for amendments, including destructive changes.
  • Answer scenario-style interview questions, such as building an oncology cycle or handling a mid-study amendment.

Constraints

  • Two days: prioritize likely interview topics; defer deep administration and integration topics.
  • No Vault EDC environment: use Veeva documentation and simulated exercises.
  • Existing EDC experience: do not reteach CRF design or general clinical data management basics.

Out of scope for now

Vault CDB (Clinical Database) and Workbench, CDMS API and integrations, Vault Payments, Labs administration, and randomization and supply (RTSM) internals. Formal Veeva certification preparation can follow the interview.
